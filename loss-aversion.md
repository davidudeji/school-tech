# UNIVERSAL LOSS-AVERSION FRAMEWORK

Loss aversion should NOT mean frightening users.

Use this hierarchy:

## LEVEL 1 — INFORMATIONAL URGENCY

Best for most websites.

Examples:

`Limited availability`

`Next batch begins soon`

`Applications currently open`

`Booking window available`

`Current pricing period`

---

## LEVEL 2 — REAL SCARCITY

Use only when scarcity is genuine.

Examples:

`8 rooms remaining`

`12 units available`

`Limited production batch`

`Applications close Friday`

`Current inventory: 6`

The underlying number or deadline must come from real data.

---

## LEVEL 3 — TIME-SENSITIVE OPPORTUNITY

Use when a genuine deadline exists.

Examples:

`Early-access pricing ends Friday`

`Applications close at 5 PM`

`Booking window closes Sunday`

`Current allocation ends this month`

Never create fake countdown timers.

---

## LEVEL 4 — PERSONALIZED LOSS AVERSION

Remind users what they already started.

Examples:

`Your plan is ready`

`Your application is 80% complete`

`Your selected products are saved`

`Your booking details are ready`

`Your personalized recommendations are waiting`

This is generally safer and more trustworthy than manufacturing fear.
